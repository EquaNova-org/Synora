Synora is an agentic AI health assistant designed to improve employee well-being through continuous, privacy-conscious health monitoring and intelligent decision support.

The long-term vision is a compact AI-powered workplace device capable of monitoring non-invasive physiological and environmental indicators throughout the workday, enabling early detection of fatigue, stress, and other potential health concerns before they become more serious.

Unlike conventional AI assistants that rely on a single model, Synora is built around a multi-agent architecture, where specialized AI agents collaborate to perform different tasks. An Orchestrator Agent coordinates the workflow, while dedicated agents analyze incoming health data, interpret sensor readings, retrieve relevant medical knowledge, generate personalized recommendations, monitor user conditions over time, and securely communicate insights between the device, users, and organizational dashboards. This modular approach enables more accurate reasoning, greater scalability, and easier integration of new capabilities as the system evolves.

The current MVP focuses on Synora's intelligence layer, combining semantic search, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and medical knowledge retrieval to deliver evidence-based answers with transparent citations to their original medical sources. This foundation establishes the reasoning engine that future monitoring agents will rely on when interpreting health data and generating recommendations.

Future versions of Synora will integrate with dedicated workplace hardware and wearable devices, enabling autonomous health monitoring, proactive wellness guidance, early risk detection, and secure organizational health analytics while preserving employee privacy.
