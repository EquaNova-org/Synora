# Synora

### AI-Powered Workplace Health & Safety Infrastructure

## Overview

Synora is an AI-powered workplace wellness and emergency response platform designed to protect employee health in real time. The system continuously monitors posture, stress indicators, fatigue, and critical health signals within office environments to improve employee well-being, reduce workplace risk, and provide instant emergency support when needed.

Built for modern companies, Synora combines artificial intelligence, workplace analytics, and health-focused automation into a single enterprise solution.

The mission of Synora is simple:

> Create safer, healthier, and more human-centered workplaces using AI.

---

# The Problem

Modern workplaces face growing challenges related to:

* Employee burnout and stress
* Poor posture and long-term physical strain
* Undetected medical emergencies during work hours
* Mental fatigue caused by intensive office environments
* Lack of proactive workplace wellness systems
* Delayed emergency response in isolated workspaces

Many employees spend long hours at desks without real-time health monitoring or preventive support. In emergency situations, workers may not receive immediate attention, especially in large office environments.

---

# The Solution

Synora acts as an intelligent workplace health assistant installed within office environments.

Using AI-driven monitoring systems, Synora helps companies:

* Detect unhealthy posture and prolonged physical strain
* Monitor workplace fatigue and stress indicators
* Analyze employee wellness patterns anonymously and ethically
* Trigger real-time emergency alerts during critical incidents
* Notify nearby coworkers for first-aid assistance
* Automatically contact emergency services when necessary
* Promote healthier and safer working environments

The platform is designed with a privacy-first architecture that prioritizes consent, security, and ethical AI usage.

---

# Core Features

## AI Posture Detection

Computer vision models analyze employee posture and provide corrective wellness recommendations to reduce physical strain and long-term health risks.

## Workplace Wellness Monitoring

AI systems identify signs of fatigue, stress, and unhealthy work patterns during intensive office hours.

## Emergency Detection & Response

Synora can detect critical health-related incidents and automatically:

* Send emergency alerts
* Notify nearby staff
* Escalate emergency protocols
* Contact medical services when required

## Health Analytics Dashboard

Companies receive high-level workplace wellness insights and risk analysis without compromising employee privacy.

## Privacy-First Infrastructure

Synora is designed with:

* encrypted data handling,
* secure enterprise infrastructure,
* ethical AI policies,
* and consent-based monitoring.
